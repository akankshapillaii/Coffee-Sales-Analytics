## Project Overview

This project analyzes **1,000 coffee orders** to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product preferences, and market trends.

The analysis transforms raw order, customer, and product data into an **interactive Excel dashboard** designed to help stakeholders quickly identify sales drivers, high-value customers, and areas of opportunity.

### Business Objectives

* Track overall **sales and order performance**
* Identify **top-performing coffee products and categories**
* Compare sales across **countries and customer segments**
* Analyze **customer purchasing behavior and loyalty**
* Identify trends that can support **sales and marketing decisions**



## Dataset

The workbook contains multiple interconnected datasets:

| Dataset          | Purpose                                                     |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Orders**       | Transaction-level sales and order information               |
| **Customers**    | Customer demographics and loyalty information               |
| **Products**     | Product pricing, coffee type, roast, size and profitability |
| **Orders-Final** | Cleaned and enriched analysis-ready dataset                 |

### Key Fields

`Order Date` · `Customer ID` · `Product ID` · `Country` · `Coffee Type` · `Roast Type` · `Size` · `Quantity` · `Unit Price` · `Sales` · `Loyalty Card`

## 💡 Key Insights

* **$45.1K** in total sales were generated across the analyzed orders.
* **United States** contributed the largest share of sales at approximately **$35.6K**.
* **Excelsa** generated the highest sales among coffee types at approximately **$12.3K**.
* **Large roast/size category** recorded the highest sales among the analyzed roast segments at approximately **$17.4K**.
* Loyalty-card customers generated approximately **$21.2K** in sales, providing a useful basis for evaluating customer retention and loyalty initiatives.

---

## 📈 Business Takeaways

The analysis highlights opportunities to:

* Focus marketing efforts on **high-performing coffee types and markets**.
* Investigate the purchasing behavior of **loyalty-card customers** to strengthen retention.
* Explore growth opportunities in lower-performing countries and product segments.
* Use customer-level sales data to identify and target **high-value customers**.
* Monitor product and market performance regularly to support data-driven decisions.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

* The dataset contains **1,000 order records**, limiting broader market-level conclusions.
* Customer email information contains missing values.
* The analysis is based on historical transaction data and does not include external factors such as competitor activity, promotions, or customer acquisition costs.
